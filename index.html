About Me

I am a Senior Information Security Analyst specializing in cybersecurity policy, governance, and regulatory compliance. At the University of Utah, I translate FISMA, NIST SP 800-53, CMMC, NIST SP 800-171, FedRAMP, and CUI requirements into practical controls for IT systems and research environments.

My background combines security program leadership, vulnerability and patch management, access control, incident readiness, and hands-on infrastructure operations. In my free time, I enjoy spending time with family, riding and fixing motorcycles, and working in my Proxmox-based home lab.
